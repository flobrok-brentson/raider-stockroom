Raider Stockroom

Set how many of each crafted item you want. Enter what you hold. The loot list reorders itself to show what to go after next.

Item data comes from MetaForge's item list, the ARC Data Project (github.com/RaidTheory/arcraiders-data, MIT license) and arctracker.io, with a few recipes checked against arcraiders.wiki. It was last updated mid-2026, so newer patches may differ. Check anything that looks off at the in-game workbench. ARC Raiders and its items are the property of Embark Studios AB. Yields from loot can vary with rarity.
