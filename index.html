Raider Stockroom

Set how many of each crafted item you want. Enter what you hold. The loot list reorders itself to show what to go after next.

Recipes and break-down yields come from the community item databases (arcraiders.wiki and metaforge.app) as of mid-October 2026. A few entries disagree between sources, or change with patches, so check anything marked "verify" at the in-game workshop. Yields from loot can vary with rarity.
